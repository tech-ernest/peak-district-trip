Photography Spots

📸

Lud's Church

An 18m-deep moss-covered chasm near Gradbach — otherworldly light filters through the rock walls. ~30 min walk from the car park. Best on overcast days or at sunrise/sunset.

📸

Winnats Pass

Dramatic limestone gorge near Castleton. One of the most iconic shots in the Peak District — best at sunrise when mist fills the valley below.

📸

Mam Tor Summit

Panoramic views across Hope Valley and Edale. Famous for cloud inversions on still mornings — catch a sea of mist with hilltops poking through.

📸

Padley Gorge

Ancient oak woodland with a rocky stream running through — ideal for long-exposure waterfall shots and dappled light through the canopy.

📸

Chrome Hill & The Dragon's Back

A distinctive limestone ridge near Earl Sterndale — the twin peaks of Chrome Hill and Parkhouse Hill make for a striking sunrise silhouette.

🪞

Stanage Edge Portrait

A 4-mile gritstone escarpment above Hathersage — one of the most popular portrait and pre-wedding shoot locations in the Peak District. Dramatic cliffs, wide moorland sky, golden hour light. ~30 min drive.

🪞

Dovedale Stepping Stones Portrait

16 limestone stepping stones across the River Dove surrounded by lush valley scenery — beautiful, accessible, and incredibly photogenic. Best early morning before crowds arrive. ~35 min drive.
